Welcome to the Value4Farm Audit Tool

Part of the Value4Farm Decision Support Framework. This project has received funding from the European Union's Horizon Europe Research and Innovation programme under Grant Agreement No. 101116076 (VALUE4FARM)

See if combining solar panels, biogas, and farming works for your farm. By answering a series of questions, this tool audits the potential to implement solar eneryg and/or biogas at your farm, considering the legal, economic, agronomic, social, and environmental aspects and impacts.

✔ Purely Informative

Calculations rely on scientific research from the Value4Farm project and published data. This tool does not make final decisions for you.

✔ Fully Anonymous

Your privacy matters. All inputs are processed strictly in real-time to generate your report. No data is saved or stored.

The 3-Step Transition Framework:

  1. 1. Learn: Complete the introductory online course at https://www.futurelearn.com/courses/integrating-food-and-energy-production-on-farmland to understand the core concepts.
  2. 2. Audit: (Current Step) Evaluate your farm's compatibility.
  3. 3. Transition: Access the implementation tool to find actionable deployment paths, examples, and local expert help.

This tool assesses the feasibility of combining energy production through Agrivoltaics and/or Biogas with food production. It provides a localized multi-criteria analysis based on environmental, financial, agronomic, social, and legislative aspects.

✔ Purely Informative

Your privacy matters. All inputs are processed strictly in real-time to generate your report. No data is saved or stored.
